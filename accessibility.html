Accessibility

We want everyone to enjoy their visit. Here is what to expect and how we can help.

Physical access

  • Step-free access to all public areas
  • Lift to all floors (door width of 90cm)
  • Two accessible parking bays near the entrance
  • accessible restrooms on every floor

Sensory information

  • Quiet hour every Thursday 10:00-11:00
  • Some galleries have loud sounds or flashing lights-look for signage
  • Noise-dampening headphones available to borrow
  • Quiet room near reception

Support

  • Free admission for carers/companions
  • Assistance dogs welcome (water bowls available)
  • Large-print maps at reception

Website accessibility

We follow WCAG 2.2 AA guidelines for color contrast, keyboard navigation, clear hierarchy, and alternative text for images. If you find a barrier, please let us know on the Contact page
