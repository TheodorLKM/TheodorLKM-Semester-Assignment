Exhibits

Our galleries are designed for curious minds aged 7-15. Explore space, life on earth, robotics, engineering, and hands-on creativity.

Space explorers

Launch mini-rockets, explore scale models of the solar system, and pilot a Mars-rover in our interactive space arena.

  • Rover driving simulator
  • Build and launch air rockets
  • Constellation projector

Best for ages 8+ Allow 30-40 minutes.

Life & Time

Meet fossils, explore ancient animals, and uncover how life on earth has changed across millions of years.

  • Fossil excavation pit
  • Interactive timeline wall
  • Augmented reality ancient creatures

Best for ages 7+. Great for school groups.

RoboWorks Lab

Control robotic arms, test sensors, and discover how robots help in manufacturing, healthcare and space exploration.

  • Robotic arm challenge
  • Sensor exploration table
  • Build-a-bot station

Ages 9+ High interaction exhibit.
